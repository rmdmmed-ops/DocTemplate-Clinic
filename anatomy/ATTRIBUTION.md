# Anatomical silhouette source

Source: https://github.com/HichamELBSI/react-native-body-highlighter

Copyright (c) 2022 ELABBASSI Hicham. MIT License; full text in LICENSE.txt.

Geometry from components/SvgMaleWrapper.tsx, assets/bodyFront.ts, and assets/bodyBack.ts on main, retrieved 2026-09-06. The male model outlines, head, and hair were assembled into standalone front and back SVG silhouettes. Original path coordinates were preserved; muscle detailing was omitted, fill changed to neutral gray-green, and viewBox cropped to reduce whitespace. Keep LICENSE.txt with distributed copies or substantial portions.

These are adult male reference silhouettes, not an anatomically validated medical atlas. They are intended only as a neutral region-selection interface background. Hotspots and clinical content are separate.
